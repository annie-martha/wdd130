Our Founders

If you agree with the statement above, then the army will be right up your alley. Our head speaker Dylan Hardy understands the original vision for this country. It wasn’t one meant to be forged by a dictator, it was made by the people, for the people. To restore that, would be fulfilling the one piece missing today. Founded in 2019 by Stuart and Whitney Madden, he has worked every day to honor their legacy since being assassinated.
